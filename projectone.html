The Firefly's Story: When Hands Begin to Glow

A visual story that invites children to explore Deaf identity, Filipino Sign Language, and the beauty of communicating in different ways through Alipat's glowing journey.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

What we want children to learn.

01

Promote Deaf Awareness

Help children see Deaf identity with understanding and appreciate the many ways people connect.

02

Introduce FSL

Let children discover Filipino Sign Language through Alipat's story, illustrations, and visual signs.

03

Encourage Inclusion

Encourage children to accept themselves, respect differences, and build understanding between Deaf and hearing communities.

FINAL PROJECT OUTPUTS

Bringing Alipat's story to life.

01 · Character Design

Meet Alipat through character designs that show the young Deaf firefly's appearance and expressions.

02 · Storyboard and Illustrations

Story scenes and illustrations that bring Alipat's feelings, journey, and message to life.

03 · Printed Storybook

A 20–24-page illustrated storybook that follows Alipat's journey of communication and acceptance.

04 · Digital Storybook

An online version that lets readers follow Alipat's story through digital pages.

05 · Educational Website

A visual space to explore the project, meet its creators, and discover its story and learning content.

06 · FSL Storytelling Video

A storytelling video that brings Alipat's journey to life through Filipino Sign Language and visuals.
